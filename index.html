Grower's Field Notes — Best Practices
✅
DO: Check pH daily — keep between 5.8–6.5 for coco/hydro, 6.0–7.0 for soil. Consistent readings prevent nutrient lock-out.
❌
AVOID: Don't flush unnecessarily — modern nutrients don't build up the way old-school products did. Only flush if you see salt crusting or severe pH drift.
✅
DO: Maintain VPD between 0.8–1.2 kPa in veg and 1.0–1.5 kPa in flower. VPD drives transpiration and nutrient uptake rate.
❌
AVOID: Never let humidity exceed 60% RH during flowering week 4+. Botrytis (bud rot) can devastate an entire harvest in 48–72 hours.
✅
DO: Keep CO₂ at 1000–1500 ppm during lights-on with high PPFD. Below 800 ppm at 600+ PPFD, you're leaving yield on the table.
❌
AVOID: Don't harvest based on calendar alone. Check trichomes under 60–100× magnification. Cloudy = peak THC, amber = degraded. Aim for mostly cloudy, some amber.
